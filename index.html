About the workshop

Replication is becoming part of the review process at a growing number of journals in operations research and the management sciences. Some journals already have a replication review in place; others are just beginning to consider one. Each has had to build its process largely from scratch.

This workshop is meant to change that. The goal is to share experience and to develop shared guidance and procedures, so that journals do not each have to reinvent this wheel. Ideally, a written report will come out of the workshop that can serve as a guide for editors just starting down this road.

Because journals are at very different stages of adoption, we expect a varied group — and varied opinions. The hope is that discussing the issues together yields a common vision of where this should go.

This is the second workshop on the topic. The first was held at an INFORMS Annual Meeting, with editors from four INFORMS journals.

Topics we expect to cover

  • Designing a replication review that fits an existing editorial process
  • Which papers to replicate, and how to decide
  • Finding replicators, and what to ask of them
  • Tools, infrastructure, and reproducibility standards
  • Reporting results, and handling disagreements
  • Cost, time, and the load on editors and authors

The workshop is built around editors of major journals in operations research and the management sciences, with authors and others in the review ecosystem taking part as well. Cross-flow between editors and authors is very much the point.
